# Sales Performance Dashboard

## 1. Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, product trends, profit margins, and regional distribution to support strategic business decisions.

## 2.Description
The Sales Performance Pro Dashboard is a dynamic Power BI report built to monitor and evaluate company-wide sales performance across products, countries, and years. It provides insights into total sales, profit margins, order volume, and product line performance, enabling data-driven decision-making for revenue growth and operational optimization.

## 3. Tech Stack
The dashboard was developed using:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization and reporting platform

🔄 Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation

🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Custom measures for Total Sales, Profit Margin, Order Count, and KPIs

🗂 Data Modeling – Relationships between orders, products, customers, and geographic dimensions

📁 File Format – .pbix for development and dashboard deployment

## 4. Data Source
Source: Kaggle

Order details (Order Number, Quantity Ordered, Price Each)

Product information (Product Name, Product Line)

Customer details (Country, City)

Sales and Profit metrics

Order year (2003–2005)

The data is structured across related tables such as orders, order details, products, and customers to enable cross-filtering and aggregated analysis.

## 5. Highlights

• Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to quickly understand:

Which product lines generate the most revenue?

Which countries contribute the highest profit?


• Goal of the Dashboard

To create an interactive analytical tool that:

Tracks overall sales and profit performance


## 6. • Business Impact & Insights

✅ Revenue Optimization
Identifies high-performing product lines (e.g., Classic Cars) to prioritize marketing and inventory strategies.

✅ Market Expansion Strategy
Country-level insights support geographic expansion and regional targeting.

✅ Profitability Analysis
Helps evaluate whether high sales volume aligns with strong profit margins.

✅ Product Strategy Improvement
Pinpoints low-performing products for pricing review or promotional campaigns.

✅ Executive Decision Support
Provides leadership with a clear overview of financial health and sales trends.
